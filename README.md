# Loose Leaf
A digital book-maker and note-keeper with turnable pages, customizable fields and stickers, and templates for a variety of pages including: recipes, character sheets, curriculum, article, and more. 

## Why did I create Loose Leaf?

I am a big lover of creative freedom and physical books. I wanted to be able to make beautiful pages and books like I see in the physical world, while having the convenience of staying digital. Since being digital allows the benefits of not needing physical storage and carrying around, as well as queryability.
